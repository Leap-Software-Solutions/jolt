Add `Stepper` for ordered build, review, approval, and onboarding progress. Support horizontal and vertical layouts, complete/current/pending/error states, optional descriptions, accessible current-step and status announcements, and optional `href` or `onStepActivate` navigation. Complete steps use the success colour, the current step uses info, horizontal connectors align to the step markers regardless of description length, vertical connectors sit outside linked steps, meet the markers, and are not pointer targets, vertical spacing sits on the list item rather than inside the interactive step, and linked steps highlight as one surface using the ghost button hover colours.
